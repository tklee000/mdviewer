MdViewer HWPX export notice
===========================

본 제품은 한컴의 HWP 문서 파일(.hwp) 공개 문서를 참고하여 개발하였습니다.

The HWPX exporter implements the OWPML package format standardized as
KS X 6101 and was developed with reference to Hancom's published document
format materials and the Apache-2.0-licensed hancom-io/hwpx-owpml-model
project. MdViewer is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hancom Inc.

https://www.hancom.com/support/downloadCenter/hwpOwpml
